Model brand icon notices

The PNG files drawable/model_*.png are offline renderings of the SVG sources
packaged in this directory. Their source paths, pinned revisions, SHA-256 hashes,
and comparison evidence are recorded per asset in provenance.json. The original
MIT upstream SVG evidence is retained under upstream/.

These brand illustrations originate from LobeHub Icons:
https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons
Copyright (c) 2023 LobeHub. Licensed under MIT; see LobeHub-MIT-LICENSE.txt.
Brand names and logos remain the trademarks of their respective owners.

Kelivo (https://github.com/Chevey339/kelivo) is AGPL-3.0 licensed. No Kelivo
application code is included. Ten visual SVGs follow its assets/icons collection
and have equivalent MIT-licensed LobeHub source evidence: DeepSeek, Claude,
OpenAI (historical LobeHub version), Gemini, Qwen (historical version), Grok,
MiniMax, Mistral, Gemma, and Meta. Differences include XML whitespace, generated
gradient identifiers, and Meta's redundant grouping wrapper. DeepSeek retains
the original #4D6BFE blue whale.

The provenance of Kelivo's codex.svg and kimi-color.svg could not be established
as MIT. Those two Kelivo SVGs are NOT distributed here. Instead, the packaged
Codex and Kimi resources use explicitly MIT-licensed LobeHub same-brand icons;
the manifest documents this deliberate difference from the Kelivo prototype.

Assets are rendered once at development time into transparent 192x192 PNGs.
No network, SVG parser, or color tint is used to display these images at runtime.
Monochrome SVG currentColor uses the SVG default black. The project's own
license is not changed by inclusion of these separately licensed assets.
